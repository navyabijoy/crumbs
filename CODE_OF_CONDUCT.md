# Code of Conduct

crumbs follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). Please read it before taking part.

In short: be welcoming, especially to people making their first open source contribution. Be patient with questions, give feedback on the work rather than the person, and assume good intent. Harassment, insults, and personal or political attacks are not accepted in any project space, including issues, pull requests and discussions.

## Reporting

If you see or experience behaviour that breaks this code, contact the maintainer, [@navyabijoy](https://github.com/navyabijoy), privately. Reports are handled confidentially. The maintainer may remove comments, close threads, or block anyone who breaks this code, following the enforcement guidelines in the Contributor Covenant.
